About the founder

Sameer Wagh

Sameer founded SecretBit Ventures to work on problems that cross agentic AI, privacy and cryptography, and takes them from research question to running system.

In numbers

{% include stats.html %}

Sameer is also a Senior Research Scientist at OpenMined and a Visiting Researcher at the University of Virginia. Before that he led research and development at Devron, turning ideas such as vertical federated learning and AI-driven privacy controls into shipped product.

Sameer spent nearly a decade in research: a postdoc at UC Berkeley’s RISE Lab, a PhD at Princeton, three stints at Microsoft Research (Redmond, Bangalore and Cambridge) and a B.Tech. with Honors in Engineering Physics from IIT Madras. The throughline is computing on data without ever seeing it, and making that practical enough to deploy.

At OpenMined, his work includes secure enclaves for AI evaluation with Anthropic and the UK AI Safety Institute, double-blind evaluations with Google DeepMind, and secure aggregation across United Nations agencies.
